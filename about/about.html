About us

Prescription GI care, designed by a doctor

The Gastronomy Project makes doctor-reviewed, compounded medications for the gut, each one pairing a prescription-strength medicine with ginger root extract in a single easy formulation. Built by a doctor who wanted GI care that was simpler, more honest, and actually pleasant to use.

From the founder

Why I built The Gastronomy Project

I am Ryan Natale, a physician. I spent years watching people struggle with the same gut problems, nausea, heartburn, and reflux, and settle for either a drawer full of drugstore bottles or a prescription that was hard to get with no support around it.

I wanted something better: a prescription-strength medication and ginger root extract, together in one thoughtfully made formulation, reviewed by a real doctor and delivered without the runaround. That is The Gastronomy Project. I take these myself, and I built the company I wish existed when I needed it.

Ryan Natale, MD · Founder · Why we chose ginger

How it works

Who actually handles your care

We are honest about who does what. The Gastronomy Project designs the products and runs the experience. Independent, licensed partners handle the medicine.

The Gastronomy Project

We create the formulas and run the storefront, your plan, and support. We are not the prescriber or the pharmacy, and we never make a prescribing decision.

Your doctors: MD Integrations

Your intake is reviewed and prescribed by U.S.-licensed doctors through MD Integrations, an independent telehealth physician network operating in all 50 states.

Your pharmacy: Curexa East

Your medication is compounded and shipped by Curexa East, a licensed U.S. compounding pharmacy. One doctor review, then free shipping to your door.
